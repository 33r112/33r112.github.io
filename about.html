About

About Me

I'm ryoku33r — I make things, mostly games and the occasional tool, and I keep a running record of what I play, watch and read. This site is where that record lives.

Interests

RPGs — turn-based and otherwise — game design and systems, Unreal Engine, old web culture, and whatever anime or manga I've most recently gotten attached to.

What THREAD 33 Is

THREAD 33 is a personal archive, not a portfolio and not a blog. Library keeps track of games, movies, TV, anime and manga I've gone through. Projects holds things I've built. Notes is where ideas worth keeping — mostly about game design and development — get written down instead of lost. It's meant to be added to for a long time, not launched once and abandoned.
