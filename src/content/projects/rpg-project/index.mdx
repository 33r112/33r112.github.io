A turn-based × action combat RPG built in Unreal Engine 5. Combat blends real-time positioning with a turn-based skill system — currently prototyping the skill-slot design and encounter flow.

The core loop is built around a small hand of equipped skills rather than a long menu of spells: fewer choices, but each one meaningfully changes positioning on the field. Movement happens in real time between turns, so lining up an enemy for a follow-up isn't just a stat check — it's something the player actually has to read and react to, closer to an action game's spacing than a traditional turn-based encounter.

Encounter design is the other half of the prototype. Early tests leaned too far toward "one big arena, fight until someone's dead," which made the positioning system feel decorative rather than load-bearing. The current pass breaks fights into smaller beats — cover, chokepoints, staggered enemy spawns — so that where you stand going into a turn matters as much as what you queue up.

Skill-slot design is still the part getting rewritten the most. Slots need to feel distinct without ballooning into a spreadsheet of edge cases, and the goal is to keep the whole kit readable at a glance rather than requiring a wiki to parse. That balance — depth without bloat — is the thing this prototype phase exists to find before any content gets built on top of it.
